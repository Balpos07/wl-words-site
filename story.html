About this story

Adaugo has spent five years rebuilding her life after a broken engagement nearly destroyed her faith and her sense of self. Now a celebrated wedding planner in Lagos, she keeps love at a careful distance — until a client assigns her to plan the wedding of the one man who got away.

Emeka Nwosu has never stopped believing that God brings second chances. But convincing Adaugo that their story isn't finished will take more than words — it will take a miracle, the kind that only comes when two stubborn hearts finally let go and trust.

Set across Lagos and Abuja, The Faithful Heart is a tender story about what happens when faith is tested not by tragedy, but by hope.
