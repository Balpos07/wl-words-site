Frequently Asked Questions

Do coins expire? ▼
No. Your coins never expire. Once you buy them, they stay in your account until you choose to spend them — whether that's tomorrow or two years from now.
What happens when I unlock a chapter? ▼
Once you spend coins to unlock a chapter, it's yours permanently. You can read it as many times as you want, on any device, without spending coins again. Just log in and it's there.
How much of my purchase goes to the writer? ▼
80% of every coin you spend on a story goes directly to that writer. W&L Words takes only 20% to cover platform costs. We believe writers should be paid well for their gift.
Can I get a refund on my coins? ▼
Unused coins can be refunded within 7 days of purchase. Once coins have been spent to unlock chapters, those specific coins cannot be refunded. Contact support@wlwords.com for refund requests.
Is my payment information safe? ▼
Yes. We never store your card details on our servers. All payments are processed by Stripe (international) or Paystack (Nigeria/Africa), both of which are PCI-DSS compliant and trusted by millions of businesses worldwide.
Can I buy coins as a gift for someone else? ▼
Gift coins are coming soon! We're building a gifting feature so you can send coins to friends and family. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when it launches.
